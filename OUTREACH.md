# Outreach instructions

Use the editable template from the CRM (`python3 tools/outreach.py template`) as the basis for each draft. The template describes Cerato's offer; sender details come from Settings. The signature is handled separately when sending. Choose an appropriate subject if none is set.

Research the company's actual website and save concise verified findings and their source URLs. Write a natural personalized sentence grounded in those findings. Do not invent company facts or claim a prior relationship. Use the contact name and email already on the lead without checking whether the address belongs to the named contact. Use the listed contact name for the greeting and replace each template placeholder before creating a finished draft. Keep the email concise and preserve the intent of the template. Respect opt-outs.

Lead outreach is draft-only. Do not create simulated-send records or send actual outreach emails. The user may manually compose and confirm real emails in Inbox when explicitly requested.

Personalization: research the company’s own website, public LinkedIn activity, and credible news. Prefer a specific relevant development from the last 12 months; if none is available, use a distinctive operational detail with a clear source. Connect the signal to a specific booking, data-entry, document or shipment-update workflow; avoid an opening that could fit another carrier after swapping its name or route. Use a natural observation, question, or both; make unverified friction explicitly uncertain. Write a concise, natural opening that fits the rest of the template without restating Cerato’s offer or assuming the prospect has a problem. Do not present old or undated events as recent. If nothing meaningful is supported, use a neutral observation or question without invented company facts and flag the weak evidence for review.
